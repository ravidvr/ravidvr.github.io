1. Data Collection

This website does not collect, store, or process any personal data. There are:

  • No cookies
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No user accounts or login
  • No form submissions that store data on a server

2. Location Data

If you choose to use the "Use my location" feature, your browser's geolocation is processed entirely on your device (client-side only). Your coordinates are never sent to or stored on any server. They are saved only in your browser's local storage for your convenience and can be cleared at any time.

3. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical access logs as part of their infrastructure. See GitHub Pages Data Collection.

4. Third-Party Services

The map tiles are served by CARTO. Search functionality (Fuse.js) and mapping (Leaflet.js) run entirely in your browser. No data is sent to external APIs during use.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR (DSGVO), you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your personal data. Since this site collects none, there is nothing to exercise these rights on. For questions, contact the operator via the GitHub repository.
