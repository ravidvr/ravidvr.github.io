About BerlinDeals Map

A free, public web app that shows weekly supermarket promotional offers on an interactive map of Berlin. No accounts, no tracking, no cookies.

How It Works

  1. Store locations come from OpenStreetMap under the ODbL open data license. Map tiles by CARTO.
  2. Offer data is collected from supermarket chain websites' publicly accessible offer pages — logged out, robots.txt compliant, rate-limited. Only factual data is collected (product names, prices, dates).
  3. The dashboard is a static HTML file generated by a Python pipeline. Search runs entirely in your browser using Fuse.js fuzzy matching.

Legal

Under EU law (Database Directive 96/9/EC), factual data such as product names, prices, and dates are not copyrightable. Every offer links back to its original source on the chain's website. The project is open source under the MIT license.

Store markers use chain-branded colors and text initials — no trademarked logos are reproduced.

Limitations

  • Offers are chain-wide — a product on offer at "Rewe" is shown at all Berlin Rewe locations. Individual stores may not stock every offer.
  • Prices are scraped from public web pages and may not reflect in-store pricing. Always confirm at the store.
  • This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any supermarket chain.
